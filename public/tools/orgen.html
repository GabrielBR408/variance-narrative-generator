What it solves

Owner reporting is a compilation problem disguised as a writing problem. The financial data is usually already correct — it's sitting in your income statement, your budget, your GL. The actual work is stitching it into the format a specific owner expects, month after month, without introducing errors or inconsistencies along the way.

Doing this by hand means opening last month's PDF, using it as a visual template, manually re-entering or copy-pasting this month's figures, checking that totals tie out, and formatting commentary to match the established structure. It's tedious, and tedious work is exactly where copy-paste errors sneak in — a number that doesn't update, a total that's off by one row, a section that gets skipped because the report has grown to include an unusual number of moving parts.

The Owner Report Generator handles the compilation: give it your prior-month report as the format reference and your current-month data, and it produces the next report in that same structure. Critically, the income-statement parsing is deterministic — meaning the LLM in this tool never touches or interprets your financial figures. Numbers go in, numbers come out unchanged, formatted into the report. The AI's job is limited to compiling and structuring, not deciding what your P&L says.

FAQ

Does the AI ever change or estimate my financial numbers?
No. The income-statement parsing is deterministic — the LLM is not involved in interpreting or generating the numbers. It handles formatting and compilation; your source data determines every figure.
What if this month's report needs a section the prior report didn't have?
The tool works best when matching an existing format closely. For structural changes — a new section, a different table layout — plan on a manual adjustment pass after generation, at least until that new format has run once as the "prior report" reference.
How reliable is this, really?
346 out of 346 regression tests are currently passing. That's not a claim of perfection, but it reflects a tool that's been tested against a wide range of real report scenarios, not just the happy path.
Do I still need to review the output before sending it to an owner?
Yes — always. This tool compiles the report; it doesn't replace a final human review before something goes to an owner or asset manager.
What file formats does it accept?
The prior-month report is provided as a PDF; current-month data comes from your income statement and budget exports.
Is this a prototype or something I can rely on month to month?
It's a stable, production tool with a strong regression-testing track record — built to be part of your actual reporting cycle, not a proof-of-concept.

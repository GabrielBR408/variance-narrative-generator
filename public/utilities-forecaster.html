⚠ CAM (Common Area Maintenance) is the shared building cost billed back to tenants. These percentages describe the whole building's blended consumption in a master-metered building (one meter for the whole building, not per-suite), not one tenant's suite. Changing the mix (e.g. converting one floor to lab/medical) changes the blended cost — and therefore the CAM cost billed to every tenant, not just the space that changed. For true tenant-by-tenant CAM billing, each tenant's suite needs its own meter ("submetering") or a separate model.
